The V29.6 QA failed because the popup was still physically inside the hero DOM tree. A transformed/backdrop-filter ancestor changed the containing block, so position:fixed rendered at panel_top=28 while hero_bottom=192. The date panel therefore intersected the hero.

Apply this exact patch:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mohamedamouseo-a11y/67-patchs/main/67-admin-v29.6.1-external-date-portal-fix-apply.sh

GOAL
- Keep the date trigger exactly where it is inside the hero toolbar.
- Render only the opened .executive-date-popover through React createPortal(..., document.body).
- The date panel must appear fully BELOW and OUTSIDE the hero rectangle.
- The date panel must float over page content without increasing hero height or shifting KPI layout.
- Restore the full V29.5 dark/gold luxury material treatment.
- Preserve the current high-res hero, hero geometry, date logic, data, backend, auth and sidebar.
